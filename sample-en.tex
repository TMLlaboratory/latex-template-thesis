\begin{abstract}
This dissertation studies multiclass classification based on
error-correcting output codes (ECOC). We analyze the classification error of
ECOC classifiers and show conditions under which the code-word table attains
the optimal error rate. Experiments on text classification support the
analysis.
\end{abstract}
